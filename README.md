### 👋 A bit about me

I'm an Information Science undergrad who ended up spending most of my free time in the AI/ML rabbit hole — mostly LLM systems, retrieval, and agents. I like taking an idea from "what if" to something that actually runs, gets evaluated, and doesn't fall over in production. Outside of that, I compete in hackathons and grind DSA because I genuinely enjoy the problem-solving.

- 🔭 Currently deep in: multi-agent orchestration and evaluation tooling
- 🌱 Learning more about: cost-aware model routing and observability for LLM apps
- 🏆 Recently: podium finishes at HackAthena, ADVAYA, and a few campus hackathons
- ⚡ Fun fact: I'd rather debug a broken agent loop at 1 AM than leave it for tomorrow
